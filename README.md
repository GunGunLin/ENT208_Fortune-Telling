# CyberProphet (赛博先知) 
## Immersive Digital Metaphysics Platform | 沉浸式数字命理平台

**ENT208TC Industry Readiness - Project Brief** **Session 2 — Group 20**

---

## 1. Problem Statement | 问题陈述
Young adults (aged 18–30) currently face chronic anxiety regarding academics, careers, and relationships. Traditional metaphysics services are often:
* **Inaccessible**: Cumbersome and expensive offline processes.
* **Outdated**: Cultural expressions that clash with modern aesthetics.
* **Superficial**: Social media predictions lack depth and personalized logic.

当今 18-30 岁的年轻人普遍面临学业、职业和人际关系的慢性焦虑。传统的命理服务往往存在以下问题：
* **难以触及**：线下流程繁琐且昂贵。
* **表现过时**：文化表现形式与现代审美脱节。
* **缺乏深度**：社交媒体上的星座预测过于碎片化，缺乏个性化逻辑和深度反馈。

---

## 2. Target Users | 目标用户
Designed for **Digital Natives** and **Early-Career Professionals**:
* **Psychographic**: Logical yet open to metaphysical guidance; seeking psychological comfort and self-reflection.
* **Pain Points**: Desire a private, immersive space to gain culturally deep and ritualistic insights quickly.

本项目专为**数字化原住民**和**职场新人**设计：
* **心理画像**：重视逻辑推理，同时也对玄学引导持开放态度；寻求心理慰藉与自我反思。
* **用户诉求**：渴望在一个私密、具有沉浸感的数字空间中，快速获得具有文化深度和仪式感的指引。

---

## 3. The Solution | 解决方案
**CyberProphet** is a modern digital metaphysics platform using a **"Cyberpunk meets Zen"** visual aesthetic. It acts as a "digital confidant," transforming metaphysical data into actionable insights.

**CyberProphet (赛博先知)** 是一款现代化的数字命理平台，采用**“赛博朋克碰撞禅意”**的视觉美学。它充当用户的“数字知己”，将复杂的形而上学数据转化为可操作的洞察。

### Key Features | 核心功能
* **Cyber-Zen Immersive Interface**: High-fidelity visual environments that provide a sense of "digital ritual."
* **Dynamic Integrated Analysis**: Tailored insights combining multiple divination logics for a holistic view.
* **Interactive Decision Compass**: Tools to help users weigh options aligning with their personal "luck cycles."
* **Private Reflection Log**: A secure space to track emotional shifts and past predictions.

* **赛博禅意沉浸界面**：提供极具“数字仪式感”的高保真视觉环境。
* **动态综合分析报告**：结合多种命理逻辑，提供全方位的查询反馈。
* **交互式决策指南**：协助用户根据个人“运势周期”权衡并做出选择。
* **私密反思日志**：安全记录情绪变化与预测记录的专属空间。

---

## 4. Success Metrics | 成功指标
* **Usability**: 90% of test users complete a full consultation cycle in under 3 minutes.
* **Adoption**: At least 35% of first-time users return within 7 days.
* **Testable Feedback**: An average "Clarity and Comfort" score of at least 4.2/5 in post-testing surveys.

* **易用性**：90% 的测试用户能在 3 分钟内独立完成一次完整的咨询流程。
* **采纳率**：至少 35% 的首访用户在 7 天内回访。
* **测试反馈**：在测试调查中，“清晰度与舒适度”评分需达到 4.2/5 以上。
